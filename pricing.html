Pricing

Commonshub pricing is still being finalized.

It will be offered as a monthly or yearly service, with pricing depending in part on whether you use a Commonshub subdomain or your own custom domain or subdomain.

What’s included

Guided setup

Automated onboarding and setup for a custom-branded Mastodon community.

Hub page

A public landing page for your community with clear identity, onboarding, and support options.

Direct funding

Monthly subscriptions, yearly subscriptions, one-time tips, and fundraising campaigns through Stripe.

Payments go directly to you

Community support is processed through your Stripe account, with funds deposited directly into your account.

Hosting, maintenance, updates, and platform reliability are part of the service.
