What these are

Mastodon collections are lightweight, shareable discovery objects. Commonshub adds the transparency layer around them: why a collection exists, how it was created, what kinds of accounts it includes, and how it will improve over time.

How we curate

Trusted seeds

We begin with small sets of high-quality accounts relevant to a topic or community.

Graph discovery

We look for accounts repeatedly followed or surfaced by trusted people in related networks.

Activity filtering

Dormant accounts, noisy feeds, bridges, and obvious broadcast-only nodes are separated.

Human review

Collections are starting points, not automated rankings. Human judgment remains central.

Clear account types

Thoughtful humans, broadcasters, institutions, and utility feeds are evaluated differently.

Ongoing refinement

Collections will be updated as communities grow, feedback arrives, and the network changes.

Transparency note

Commonshub Collections are not universal rankings or endorsements. They are transparent starting points designed to help people discover useful, trustworthy accounts faster. We expect them to improve through public feedback and ongoing review.

Suggest improvements

Know an account that belongs in a collection? See one that should be removed or reclassified? We want these collections to get better in public.
