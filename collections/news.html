Why this category exists

The open social web has become an important home for independent journalists, researchers, analysts, editors, and public-interest reporting organizations. These collections are designed to help people discover thoughtful reporting, regional expertise, and durable journalism communities without relying on engagement-driven recommendation systems.

How collections are curated

Commonshub Collections are built using transparent, human-centered discovery methods. We begin with trusted seed accounts, explore overlapping social graphs, evaluate activity and account quality, and refine collections through ongoing human review.

Transparency note

These collections are intended as starting points for discovery — not universal rankings or endorsements. We expect them to evolve over time as the fediverse grows and communities mature.
