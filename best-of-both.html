The one-two punch

First show where Commonshub fits relative to other platforms. Then show how it works together with the publishing platforms people already use.

1. Where Commonshub fits

Most platforms give you reach, comments, or discovery. Commonshub is where owned relationship and higher-quality conversation come together.

2. How Commonshub works with publishing platforms

Use the publishing platforms for what they are good at: broad reach, discovery, and content distribution. Then add Commonshub for conversation, privacy, continuity, and support.

Suggested heading for SVG: Publishing platforms + Commonshub = broad reach + loyal supporters

Why this framing works

It answers the most common objection right away: why build another community when you already have followers? Because you are not starting over. You are adding the missing layer that helps part of your audience become a real community.

What publishing platforms are good for

Broad reach, discovery, and publishing written, video, and audio content. They help people find you.

What Commonshub adds

Owned relationship, better conversation, continuity beyond comment threads, and direct financial support from a smaller, more engaged core.

Why this is better for builders

You stop relying entirely on rented attention. You build something more durable, more direct, and more economically sustainable.

Why this is better for members

A calmer, more respectful place to participate. No ads. No algorithmic manipulation. Better signal and more meaningful continuity.

Use the publishing platforms for reach. Add Commonshub for community and support. Better for builders. Better for members. Better for the community itself.
