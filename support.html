Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers to the questions people are most likely to ask first.

What is Commonshub?

Commonshub gives you your own online community on your own domain — with no ads, no algorithms, and funding built in.

Do I need a custom domain?

No. You can start with a Commonshub subdomain or use your own domain if you prefer.

Does this replace my Substack or YouTube?

No. Use those for reach. Use Commonshub for the shared conversation that builds your community.

How does funding work?

Funding is powered by Stripe. Members can subscribe, tip, or contribute — and payments go directly to you.

Is it hard to set up?

No. Setup is guided and we handle the infrastructure, hosting, and updates for you.

Who owns the community?

You do. Your domain, your members, your relationship.
