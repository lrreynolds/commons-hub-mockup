Connect Stripe

Stripe will securely handle payments and send funds directly to you.

What this step does

You'll connect your community to Stripe so supporters can contribute and funds are deposited directly to your account.
Stripe handles:
• Payments from supporters
• Subscription management
• Fraud protection
• Payouts to your bank account
This usually takes 1–2 minutes and you can change everything later.

Stripe setup tips

Stripe may ask for information that isn't obvious. Here are the recommended choices for most communities.
Business type
Individual / sole proprietor
Product type
Service
Digital product
No
Business name
Use your community name
Website
Use your hub page URL
Statement descriptor
Your community name (what supporters see on their bank statement)
Support contact email
Your public contact email
Stripe will open in a new tab. You can return here at any time if you need to reference these tips.
