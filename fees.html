Transparent fees & smarter support pricing

Understand how contribution fees work and how to structure support so your community can give effectively.

Simple, transparent fees

Commonshub currently charges a 3% platform fee on community contributions.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Stripe charges standard payment processing fees, including a fixed fee per transaction. Commonshub does not hide or mark up these fees.
No hidden fees. No surprises.

You are in control

The only hard limit Commonshub imposes is a minimum contribution of $1.00.

Beyond that, you are free to structure support however you like. We provide this guidance so you understand the implications of different contribution amounts before you turn support on.

Why small payments behave differently

Payment processors charge a fixed fee on each transaction. That fixed fee has a much bigger impact on small contributions.

$1 contribution
Allowed, but the fixed processing fee represents a large share of the contribution.
$3 contribution
Still useful in some cases, but the fixed fee remains noticeable.
$5 contribution
A better minimum for one-time tips or small support payments.
$30/year instead of $3/month
Annual support is processed once instead of twelve times, which usually makes it more efficient over time.

Recommended funding setup

For most communities, we recommend starting simple and giving people a clear, low-pressure way to help.

Keep the community free to join
Let people experience the space first. Support becomes easier to ask for once the community has visible value.
Offer one-time tips of $5 or more
This keeps small contributions practical without making fees feel confusing.
Use annual support options
A good rule of thumb is to set annual support at about 10× the monthly amount. For example, $3/month could become $30/year.
Use campaigns for specific goals
Campaigns work well when you have a clear purpose, project, launch goal, or community initiative.
Avoid very small monthly subscriptions unless intentional
$1–$2/month support is allowed, but repeated fixed processing fees can reduce how much actually reaches you.

Optional fixed-fee offset

For contributions of $5.00 or less, Commonshub may ask contributors if they would like to add $0.30 to help offset the fixed fee charged by the payment processor.

This is always optional and clearly shown. We do not claim that this covers every fee or that 100% goes to the creator.

How payouts work

Contributions are handled by Stripe through your connected Stripe account.

Funds go to your connected Stripe account
Commonshub does not operate as a wallet or hold your community’s money.
Payout timing is managed by Stripe
Stripe controls payout timing based on your account, region, and payout settings. This is typically daily or weekly after Stripe’s normal processing period.

Our philosophy

Commonshub is designed to help creators and organizations build sustainable communities without hidden costs, ads, algorithmic feeds, or platform surprises.

Transparent fees. Creator control. No surprises.
