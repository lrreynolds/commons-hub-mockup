Fees & support pricing

Keep community support simple, transparent, and practical.

Simple, transparent fees

Commonshub currently charges a 3% platform fee on community contributions.

Payments are processed by Stripe through your connected Stripe account. Stripe charges standard payment processing fees, including a fixed fee per transaction.
No hidden fees. No surprises.

Pricing guidance

Commonshub allows contributions starting at $1.00, but very small transactions are less efficient because fixed payment processing fees take up a larger share.

$1–$3 contributions
Allowed, but best used intentionally because fixed fees matter more at this level.
$5+ one-time tips
A more practical starting point for small one-time support.
Annual support
Often better than tiny monthly payments. A useful rule of thumb is about 10× the monthly amount, such as $3/month or $30/year.

Recommended starting setup

For most communities, start with a simple support model and adjust later.

Keep the community free to join
Let people experience the space first. Support is easier to ask for once the value is visible.
Offer one-time tips
A simple way for people to help without committing to a subscription.
Offer yearly support
Annual support reduces repeated small transactions and can feel easier for members to understand.
Use campaigns for specific goals
Campaigns work best when there is a clear project, launch goal, or community need.

How payouts work

Funds go to your Stripe account
Commonshub does not operate as a wallet or hold your community’s money.
Payout timing is managed by Stripe
Stripe controls payout timing based on your account, region, and payout settings.

Optional fixed-fee offset

For small contributions, Commonshub may ask contributors if they want to add a small optional amount to help offset fixed payment processing fees.

This is always optional and clearly shown. We do not claim that it covers every fee or that 100% goes to the creator.

Our philosophy

Commonshub is designed to help creators and organizations build sustainable communities without hidden costs, ads, algorithmic feeds, or platform surprises.

Transparent fees. Creator control. No surprises.
