Commonshub — Financial
Status Community Financial Account

Fees & support pricing

Commonshub currently charges a 3% platform fee on community contributions. Payments are processed by Stripe through your connected Stripe account, and Stripe charges its standard processing fees.

No hidden fees. No surprises.

Recommended setup

Start simple: keep the community free to join, offer one-time tips, and use yearly support where possible.

  • $1–$3 contributions are allowed, but fixed processing fees matter more at this level.
  • $5+ is a more practical starting point for one-time tips.
  • Annual support is often better than tiny monthly payments. A useful rule of thumb is about 10× the monthly amount.
  • Campaigns work best for a clear project, launch goal, or community need.

How payouts work

Funds go to your connected Stripe account. Commonshub does not operate as a wallet or hold your community’s money. Stripe manages payout timing based on your account, region, and payout settings.

Optional fixed-fee offset

For small contributions, Commonshub may ask contributors if they want to add a small optional amount to help offset fixed processing fees. This is always optional and clearly shown.
