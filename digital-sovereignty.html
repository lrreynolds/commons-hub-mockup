What is Digital Sovereignty?

Digital sovereignty means you own the relationship with your community.

No platform in between. No algorithm deciding visibility. No system shaping behavior.

You and your community connect directly — on infrastructure you control.

What this means for you

Digital sovereignty gives you a direct, durable relationship with your community — not one mediated by platform incentives.

Own the relationship

Your community lives on your domain, not inside a platform you do not control.

Reliable communication

When you post or make an announcement, your community sees it without feed ranking or throttling.

Own your member list

You have direct access to your members, including their email.

Control the space

You set the rules, moderation standards, and tone of the community.

What this means for members

A sovereign community is also better for the people inside it.

Not the product

Members are not being tracked, profiled, or sold to advertisers.

More control

People decide what they follow and what they see without opaque ranking systems.

Better signal

Conversation is shaped by shared interest, not engagement incentives.

Continuity

Discussion builds over time instead of disappearing beneath churn and reshuffling.

Better for builders. Better for members.

Sovereignty requires sustainable funding

A direct relationship is only part of the equation. To remain independent, a community must also be economically sustainable.

When members experience real value in a healthier, more meaningful community, they are more likely to support it.

That support should flow directly to the people running the community — not be extracted by an intermediary platform.

No ads. No platform dependency. Just direct support between you and your community.

How Commonshub makes this possible

Commonshub is not just hosting. It combines a healthier social model with direct funding and managed infrastructure.

Social engine

Built on Mastodon — open, decentralized, chronological social infrastructure.

Funding engine

Powered by Stripe — memberships, tips, and contributions that flow directly to you.

Your own domain

Your community exists under your identity and control — not inside another company’s platform.

Managed infrastructure

Setup, hosting, updates, and reliability handled for you without giving up ownership.

In one sentence

Digital sovereignty means building a community you actually own — while giving members a healthier place to participate and making direct support possible.
